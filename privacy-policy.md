# The Gaslighter — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 5, 2026*

## What We Collect

The Gaslighter does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, or contact information to use the app.

When you paste a message into the app, that text is sent to our server and then to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a translation and reply suggestions. We do not store the messages you paste or the replies generated, beyond what's needed to process that single request. Nothing is saved to a database tied to you.

## Incognito Mode

The app's incognito mode changes what's shown on your screen, not what's sent to generate a response — the same processing happens either way.

## Third Parties

We use a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) to process the text you submit and generate responses. We may also use standard app infrastructure providers (such as hosting and analytics services) that process limited technical data (like crash logs or basic usage counts) to keep the app running. We do not sell your data to anyone.

## Analytics

The app may collect basic, non-identifying usage data (such as how often the app is opened, or which reply mode is used) to understand how it's being used and improve it. This data is not tied to your identity.

## Children

The Gaslighter is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data in transit (encrypted connections) and avoid retaining message content longer than necessary to generate a response. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## Your Choices

Since the app doesn't require an account or store your message history, there's no account data to request or delete. If you have questions about what's processed, contact us using the information below.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
